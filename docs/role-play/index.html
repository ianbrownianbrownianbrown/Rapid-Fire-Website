The adventure

Every quest starts with a suggestion. The rest is reckless.

Role Play, Play, Role is a fully improvised one-shot DnD-style comedy. You give us a world, a problem, and the spark of an idea. We build the adventure, make the characters, and immediately give somebody a terrible plan.

Expect bold heroes, suspicious taverns, accidental lore, wildly overconfident choices, and a whole campaign's worth of consequences in one gloriously self-contained night.
